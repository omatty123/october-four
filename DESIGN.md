# Design System: October 4

The user explicitly rejected the original oversized headings, short slogan fragments, and large empty spaces. Preserve the compact revision. The content leads: a full-sentence title, date, game situation, actual video, three real photographs, six pitches, and final result.

“Our best” refers to Jackson Chourio. “Their best” refers to San Diego's top closer, Mason Miller. Keep that exact matchup.

Use navy #101b28, deep navy #0b1420, cream #f4efdf, gold #f3c455, and supporting text #c1c8c9. The cream pitch passage uses dark text. Sunset color comes from the supplied photograph. Self-hosted Manrope supplies readable prose; Bricolage supplies modest headings and data numerals. Fonts and licenses stay under assets/fonts.

The main title is about 30px on desktop and 25px on phones. Section headings are about 22px, body text 14px, and supporting labels at least 12px. Use compact padding and content gaps. Do not use viewport-height sections or enlarge type to fill space.

On desktop, a compact column of the sunset and paired portraits sits beside the native video. On phones, the video comes first, followed by the sunset and two faces. Images have ordinary captions rather than oversized text overlays. Preserve all source photographs and complete originals.

The native video uses MLB's official 48-second highlight, playback controls, inline playback, and an English caption track. There is no autoplay. Both Watch actions scroll to the player. Keep the player at 16:9, with a fully visible ballpark poster. A media-error message provides reload and the official source.

The pitch replay updates velocity, type, description, count, and score. Pitch six changes Milwaukee's score from 2 to 4. Replay resets to pitch one. Exactly one pitch has aria-current="step". The complete pitch list and final score remain available without JavaScript. Live announcements describe pitch changes; keyboard focus is visible; reduced motion disables animation and smooth scrolling.

Keep the source disclosure compact and retain all original image links and credits. Do not introduce decorative cards, repeated park images, generic slogans, unrelated statistics, or additional interfaces.
